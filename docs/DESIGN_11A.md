## What changed

- On wide screens, the large left-aligned headline sits beside one compact composer surface. Below 760px, it stacks above the composer. Text navigation has a small amber active indicator. Paste/upload and Try an example share the top edge; Clear stays quiet. Enabled primary actions are ivory with espresso text; amber remains in brand/focus/selected accents. The optional date appears before Analyze, and the provider disclosure remains visible. Summary overview → Continue to saving → open questions leads the review; the shortcut focuses the existing save area and does not save or publish. Full evidence/editors and all existing protections remain. A short guide below the opening gives scrolling a more deliberate sequence.
- The same mounted canvas still draws 760 stable points (240 on touch/narrow screens). Each point follows a seeded 3–10px elliptical orbit plus the existing slow spatial wave, attenuated near the reading center. Scroll adds at most 8px of eased decorative drift; it never changes native scrolling or content position.
- `ui/pointerRibbon.ts` keeps up to 96 recent samples in a speed-dependent 140–840ms history (plus 80ms sampling margin). Smoothed velocity controls history length, separation, thickness and brightness. Three threads follow separate delayed time windows (10/25/45ms at full speed), are softened before Catmull–Rom interpolation, and form continuous tapered filled shapes with an ivory/muted-amber gradient. This replaces the short offset line and artificial sine wiggle. Fast turns preserve previous arcs; slow traces stay fine. Stops fade over 360ms; re-entry/tab return/scroll clear old points. The renderer reads only pointer coordinates and control type. Particle reaction stays bounded to its existing local radius.
- `ui/dotFieldCanvas.ts` owns the single elapsed-time/RAF loop, pointer listeners and observer cleanup outside React state. It caps DPR at 1.5; idle desktop draws at up to 30fps, an active desktop ribbon up to 60fps, touch/narrow fields up to 24fps. The ribbon is mouse/trackpad only, fades over editors and selections, and is disabled behind summary reading. Hidden/offscreen/inactive work pauses; reduced motion draws a static field. Guest ballot/RSVP pages mount no field or ribbon.
- `ui/presentation.ts` progressively adds 520ms/20px section entrances once per mounted section, without initially hidden content. Native wheel/trackpad/touch physics is retained; this is presentation pacing through space and a longer eased settle. The existing summary stagger stays separate and does not restart while editing. Explicit stage advancement focuses/reveals the heading; backwards stage navigation restores its recorded native scroll. Async result focus retains the guard against interrupting someone already editing elsewhere.
- Guest ballots show each option name once, emphasize the selected radio card, and keep time/location/cost, sources and checks with it. Shared plans lead with the actual activity, date/local time, meeting point and cost, followed by the three RSVP choices. Booking, instructions, links and unresolved checks remain **before** the approval checkbox and Send/Update/Reconfirm action. Status/change/cancellation notices and credential/retry logic remain unchanged.
- The HTML tab title is exactly **Fuzl**; no runtime code updates it. Active SVG favicons have no tile or background. The crescent silhouette and exactly three resting orange/yellow/ivory dots are preserved. Light-tab assets reverse the existing espresso/ivory body/smile for contrast and give the ivory fuse dot a fine silhouette edge; no icon tile outline is added. Active declarations are the SVG and a new conventional `/favicon.ico` with transparent 16/32/48px PNG frames. They use `?v=11a2` for cache invalidation. The four prior PNG variants remain transparent preview/export assets; the HTML no longer offers competing media-specific PNG links. No active manifest/Apple raster variants existed. Standalone/header geometry and the 800ms ignition remain unchanged.
