## Exact generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original panoramic landscape illustration for a contained scenic band in Fuzl's sample-plan gallery. Create a cinematic, quietly adventurous sunset over layered sandstone ridges. Gold and amber are the main palette: a low glowing amber horizon, sandy ivory haze, warm ochre rock, terracotta mid-distance ridges, and deep espresso-charcoal foreground shadows. Three to five overlapping ridge layers should create convincing atmospheric depth, with a winding valley drawing the eye inward. The sky is a soft warm gold gradient; only a very subtle smoky dusk tone in the highest corners, no dominant blue. Polished painterly editorial landscape with softly textured stone and elegant natural silhouettes, inspired by desert sunset imagery and layered landscape compositions. Wide landscape composition, roughly 3:1, clear panorama that will crop well into a shallow website band. No UI, no phone frame, no screenshot elements, no text, no logo, no watermark, no people, no buildings. Original scenery only; do not reproduce a specific place or reference artwork.
