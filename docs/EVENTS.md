## Provider choice and official documentation

One event source: **Ticketmaster Discovery API v2**, `GET https://app.ticketmaster.com/discovery/v2/events.json`. The [official Discovery documentation](https://developer.ticketmaster.com/products-and-docs/apis/discovery-api/v2/) covers US events from Ticketmaster and related ticketing sources, location/date filters, status and optional price ranges. This is a ticketing catalog, not every community event or a comprehensive local calendar. One response page (20 records) is inspected; no pagination or enrichment runs.

The [official FAQ](https://developer.ticketmaster.com/support/faq/) grants public applications 5,000 requests/day and says 2 requests/second. The Discovery reference instead says 5/second. Use the more conservative limit and your actual account quota. These official pages describe public development access rather than a paid subscription; they do **not** establish your project's billing terms or guarantee permanently free/unlimited usage. Confirm that signup/default public access requires no paid agreement in your developer account before enabling a live search. No card, billing, paid access or quota increase was configured or tested here. Fuzl caps much lower: one event action in flight, two/minute, ten attempts per API process. HTTP 429 stops with a clear quota message; no retry or fallback occurs.

Attribution uses visible **Event data: Ticketmaster** and the exact provider-supplied event/ticket link; location attribution remains Geoapify/OpenStreetMap. No images, artist creative, logos, affiliate account or purchase API are used. The [API terms](https://developer.ticketmaster.com/support/terms-of-use/) require a data-use disclosure, limit content caching, and require removal of owner-requested content within 24 hours. Fuzl displays how providers receive search inputs and that external listing privacy policies apply. Results are temporary, not saved or cached on disk. Ticketmaster's [getting-started guide](https://developer.ticketmaster.com/products-and-docs/apis/getting-started/) also links a branding guide; that linked site returned HTTP 401 during this review. No claim of branding certification or production approval is made. Review account-specific terms/branding before public release; this is a local development integration.

## Request flow and state

`PlaceFinder` owns the shared area/radius and keeps both discovery panels mounted. `EventFinder` owns separate explicit inclusive date controls and temporary results. Blank through means the same day; reversed, invalid and longer-than-31-day ranges are rejected. It captures query, summary, session/account and synchronous workspace-edit revisions; late results cannot replace newer work, including edits later reverted. Cancellation/hidden-panel changes abort and invalidate pending work. Current results move focus to their heading; errors and stale warnings appear separately.

`POST /api/private/sessions/:sessionId/events/search` uses the existing remote Supabase identity verification and owner-scoped `PlaceStore.context` read. Session revision and guest provenance are checked. Private spending is fetched internally, compared on the server and folded into general statuses. The context is checked again after retrieval; changed private replies or session revision return a conflict. The endpoint does not save or mutate the planning summary, provenance, answers, AI acceptance or review labels. Account switch/sign-out unmounts private work as before.

`GeoapifyProvider.resolve` is extracted from the existing adapter, preserving Places search behavior and fixing its resolved-area contract: clarification IDs stay in choices and are not extra fields in strict result areas. US state/city, leading-zero ZIP and ambiguous choice rules are shared. Ticketmaster gets only resolved center/geohash, radius, explicit local date range, US filter and bounded response controls. No participant, conversation, screenshot, summary, private budget or home address is sent to either provider. `TicketmasterProvider` parses optional source fields into a strict internal event DTO. Multiple venues are not silently reduced to one; venue/address/geometry remain unknown in that case. Unsupported/malformed records fail safely. Specific listing links are restricted to documented ticketing-source domains and cannot contain credential parameters.

## Fit and uncertainty rules

- Event-local dates are checked inclusively, not by truncating UTC. Date-only listings remain date-only. Reliable event/venue zones may supply the clock zone; missing/invalid/disagreeing zones, inconsistent UTC/local clocks and ambiguous/nonexistent DST times remain unresolved. Firm availability clocks are compared only when the event zone agrees with the resolved area's zone; the summary model has no participant time-zone field. Unknown end time cannot establish a fit inside a bounded firm window.
- Both `canceled` (provider spelling) and `cancelled` are excluded. Postponed/rescheduled/unknown statuses never enter verified fits. Status is retained and displayed, not replaced by “confirmed.” Onsale/offsale is not inventory confirmation.
- Reused budget comparisons require a known total, per-person basis, same known currency and a confirmed, reviewed, attributed limit. Ticketmaster's price-range minimum is always **starting**, even if min=max; no guarantee of fees or remaining tickets is inferred. Unknown budgets remain null, while zero remains zero. Currency conversion/group splitting is absent.
- Private spending only affects a general feasibility check. No exact private amounts, names, IDs, counts or hashes reach results or provider requests. As with Places, repeated feasibility queries could permit threshold inference; this is not a guarantee of anonymity against an organizer who probes arbitrary totals. Public demo mode contains no real private answers.
- Provider-reported addresses need checking; no event link is called an official venue page. Radius is a straight-line area-center boundary and permits cross-city/state results. Travel time, booking, fees and ticket availability are separate checks. Firm free-text requirements need evidence; tentative/conflicting/unreviewed facts and open questions stay unresolved.
- Up to three useful results are shown, with known exclusions separate. Active scheduled events rank ahead of postponed/rescheduled unknown-status options among unresolved candidates. A fixture can fit checked constraints; live starting prices and unverified addresses normally leave events in **Needs checking**. No reservation, attendance or confirmed-plan action exists.
